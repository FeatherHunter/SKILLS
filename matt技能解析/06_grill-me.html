03 grilling SKILL.md · 全文 12 行

--- name: grilling description: Grill the user relentlessly about a plan, decision, or idea. --- Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the decision tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one. For each question, provide your recommended answer. Ask the questions one at a time, waiting for feedback on each question before continuing. Asking multiple questions at once is bewildering. If a *fact* can be found by exploring the environment (filesystem, tools, etc.), look it up rather than asking me. The *decisions*, though, are mine — put each one to me and wait for my answer. Do not act on it until I confirm we have reached a shared understanding.

5 条核心规则

① RELENTLESS
"Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this until we reach a shared understanding."
反复访谈,直到达成共识。
② DECISION-TREE TRAVERSAL
"Walk down each branch of the decision tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one."
一根一根走决策树分支,逐条解决依赖。
③ RECOMMENDED ANSWER
"For each question, provide your recommended answer."
每个问题都要给出推荐答案,不是开放式问。
④ ONE AT A TIME
"Ask the questions one at a time, waiting for feedback before continuing."
一次一个问题。"Asking multiple questions at once is bewildering."
⑤ FACT vs DECISION
"事实自查,决策问人"。能查到的事实(file / 工具结果)就自己查,决策必须问用户。
⑥ WAIT FOR CONFIRM
"Do not act on it until I confirm we have reached a shared understanding."
用户确认前不行动。

04 决策树遍历 · 视觉化

🔵 蓝色 = 已答 · 🟢 绿 = 推荐答案可采纳 · 🔴 红 = 待答 · 橙色 = 活跃路径

关键设计:
决策被组织成树而非列表。grill 不是问 10 个独立问题,而是逐分支走,先根后叶。
推荐答案是另一个隐藏强项 —— 模型给出意见,用户只需"是 / 否 / 改成 X",而不是从零回答。

05 "Fact 自查 vs Decision 问人" · 边界设计

grilling 的第 4 段(第 10 行)划了一条非常关键的边界:

If a *fact* can be found by exploring the environment (filesystem, tools,
etc.), look it up rather than asking me. The *decisions*, though, are mine
— put each one to me and wait for my answer.

这条规则解决了什么?

类别处理方式示例
Fact(可观测) 查环境 "我们用的是 Postgres 还是 MySQL?" → 自己 cat config
Decision(用户主权) 问用户 "订单要不要支持 partial cancel?" → 一定要用户答
Recommendation(建议) 模型给推荐 "我建议选 X,因为 Y" → 用户接受/拒绝
★ 设计哲学

这条边界防止"无聊问题占用 token"。如果模型问"这个项目用什么框架?",然后用户回"React" —— 这是浪费。
能查的就查,把对话窗口留给真正需要人类判断的决策。

06 "Do not act until I confirm" · 最后一关

grilling 最后一句(第 12 行)是一道硬关卡:

Do not act on it until I confirm we have reached a shared understanding.

这不是软建议。这是 "共享理解达成" 作为行动闸门。

  • 模型不能边访谈边写代码
  • 不能边访谈边 git commit
  • 不能边访谈边调用工具
  • 只能问,等用户说"OK 我们对齐了",才能进下一阶段

这条规则让 grilling 技能天然适合作为 to-spec 的前置 —— ask-matt 主流程里 grilling-with-docs 就用同样的 primitive。

07 关键设计模式

Wrapper + Primitive Pairing · Wrapper + Primitive 配对
grill-me = 7 行 wrapper,grilling = 12 行 primitive。两个技能共同构成一个完整机制,各司其职。
Decision Tree Traversal · 决策树遍历
访谈不按列表,而按决策树的分支走。先根后叶,逐条解决依赖。
One Question at a Time
一次一个问题。"Asking multiple questions at once is bewildering" —— 这条规则保护用户认知不被淹没。
Recommended Answer · 推荐答案
每个问题都附推荐答案,把用户决策从"开放回答"变成"接受/拒绝/微调",省认知。
Fact vs Decision Boundary
明确划清"自查 vs 问人"。事实查环境,决策问用户。避免 token 浪费在无聊问题上。
Confirmation Gate · 确认闸门
"Do not act until I confirm we have reached a shared understanding." 共识未达成前不行动。
Cross-Platform Redundancy (复现)
和 ask-matt 一样,disable-model-invocation: true + allow_implicit_invocation: false 双份配置。

08 启发 · 我们可以抄什么?

★ 给访谈 / 调研技能设计的启示

1. wrapper + primitive 配对:一个技能做入口,一个技能做逻辑。
2. 一次一问:多问同时 = bewildering(用户懵)。
3. 推荐答案:把"开放问答"变成"接受/拒绝"。
4. 事实自查,决策问人:省 token,保护用户注意力。
5. 决策树遍历,不列表:先根后叶,逐条解决依赖。
6. 确认闸门:用户没确认前不动手,把访谈和行动硬分开。
7. 7 行也是有效的技能:只要它清晰指派到一个 primitive。
